# Slate 2026.11.8

- New Clock app with alarms, countdown timers, a stopwatch with laps, and reminders.
- Alarms and timers ring full screen and wake the display; reminders show a banner with a short chime.
- Notifications now appear in the quick settings drawer. Apps clear them once they no longer apply.
- Sound settings add separate volumes for media, alarms and notifications, and lower media while an alert plays.
- Settings moved from the desktop to a button next to Power in the quick settings drawer.
