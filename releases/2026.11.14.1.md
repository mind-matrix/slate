# Slate 2026.11.14.1

- Open apps: the quick settings drawer lists apps that are open or in the background. Tap one to return to it, or close it with ✕ or Close all. Voice assistants stay on and are stopped from their app details instead.
- App details can stop and start apps. Stopping a voice assistant asks first and explains what stops working.
- Settings › Apps can show all apps or only the running ones, and shows each app's status.
- New wallpapers: choose from eight built-in designs in Settings › Display › Wallpaper.
- Home in the status bar on every Settings screen, to go straight back to the desktop.
- Fixes Settings, Power, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth opening behind a full-screen app when opened from the drawer.
- Fixes an app not reopening after Back.
- Device discovery: Slate runs one shared mDNS service for apps that find and register devices on the network.
- Smoother colours on screen.
